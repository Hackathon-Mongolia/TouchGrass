# 07 · Registration

Opens Sat Sept 12. Closes Tue Oct 6. Cap at 150 registrations for 100 seats (no-shows run a third to a half). Reconfirmation Oct 3–6.

## Form fields (Google Form)

Required:

1. Full name
2. School
3. Grade
4. Date of birth
5. Email
6. Phone
7. Parent or guardian name
8. Parent or guardian phone
9. Dietary needs (none / vegetarian / halal / allergies, specify)
10. Experience: first hackathon / done one before / done several
11. Skills (multi-select): frontend, backend, mobile, design, hardware, data, other
12. Team: I have a team (list names) / I want to be matched
13. I will bring a laptop: yes / no (limited loaners)
14. I have read the rules and code of conduct: checkbox
15. Consent form: I will upload a signed form by Oct 8 or bring it to check-in: checkbox
16. Overnight: I plan to stay overnight at the venue / I will go home at 21:00 and return by 10:00 Sunday

Optional: how did you hear about us; t-shirt size if a sponsor pays for shirts; I agree to share my CV with sponsors.

## Confirmation email (auto-reply)

> You're registered for [NAME TBD], Oct 10–11 at [venue].
> Your seat is confirmed once you reply to the reconfirmation message we send on Oct 3. Seats not reconfirmed by Oct 6 go to the waitlist.
> Under 18? A parent or guardian must sign the consent form, including the overnight section: [link]. Upload it by Oct 8 or bring it signed to check-in. No form, no entry; no overnight permission, you go home at 21:00 Saturday.
> Join the group chat here: [link]. Team finding happens there and at 10:15 on Saturday.
> Questions: [email / phone].

## Waitlist

Registrations after 150 go to a waitlist in order of time. On Oct 6, fill every unconfirmed seat from the waitlist and message those people the same day.

## Reconfirmation, Oct 3–6

Message: "Reply YES to keep your seat at [NAME] on Oct 10–11. No reply by Tuesday Oct 6 at 20:00 means your seat goes to the waitlist." Send Sat Oct 3, remind Mon Oct 5.

## Check-in, Saturday 11:00–12:00

Two tables, alphabetical A–L and M–Z. Volunteer checks: name on list, consent form uploaded or handed over, overnight permission marked with a colored tag, wristband or name tag issued, dietary tag noted. Late arrivals check in at the organizer desk until 13:30.

## Data handling

Keep the sheet private to the organizing team and adult lead. Delete phone numbers and birth dates within a month after the event. Share CVs only for those who opted in.
