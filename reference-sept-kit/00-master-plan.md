# 00 · Master plan

**Event:** [NAME TBD] — a 24-hour overnight hackathon for Ulaanbaatar high school students
**Dates:** Saturday Oct 10 and Sunday Oct 11, 2026
**Hours:** 24-hour hack, Saturday 13:00 to Sunday 13:00, overnight at the venue. Doors Sat 11:00, close Sun 17:00. Plan B if no venue allows overnight: two daytime sessions, Sat 11:00–21:00 and Sun 09:00–17:00.
**Venue:** National IT Park (first ask). Fallbacks: university hall, internship company office, district Youth Development Center.
**Target:** 120 registrations, 80–110 attending, 20–30 teams, 15+ schools.
**Entry:** free.
**Money host:** a registered NGO signs sponsor contracts, receives funds, pays vendors, issues e-barimt receipts.
**Adult lead:** internship supervisor (to confirm by Sept 9).

## Why this event

- No independent, recurring, open student hackathon exists in Mongolia. Nothing independent has passed 100 people since 2021. A 100+ pupil event is the largest of its kind in the country.
- It must be finished, documented, and written up before Nov 1 (Early Decision deadline). Oct 11 leaves 20 days.

## Goals, in priority order

1. It happens, safely, with 80+ students building for 24 hours.
2. Every team demos something that runs.
3. At least three schools other than ours send teams.
4. One named sponsor and one institutional partner (venue or NGO) on the poster.
5. Outcomes recorded the same week: numbers, photos, project links, press or partner posts.

## Decisions already made

| Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Format | 24 hours with overnight, Sat 13:00 to Sun 13:00 | the most common high school format worldwide; needs venue permission for overnight access and at least three adults on site all night; Plan B is two daytime sessions |
| Theme | loose theme "Build something for students in Ulaanbaatar" plus an open track | beginners need a starting point; strong teams need freedom |
| Teams | 1–4 people | standard; keeps judging manageable |
| Fee | none | Mongolian norm; 2019 ministry directive bans fees from pupils |
| Submissions | Devpost or a Google Form with project page, demo video or live demo, repo link | free, standard |
| Judging | expo round in judge pairs, top 5 finals on stage | under 90 minutes total |
| IP | participants keep everything they build | Copyright Law: rights arise automatically, transfer only by written contract |
| Money | NGO host only; organizers never hold cash | unregistered group cannot issue receipts; under-18 cannot sign |

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Venue says yes late or cancels | two asks in parallel from day one; written confirmation; walk-through visit |
| Wifi dies at 100 laptops | test on the visit; ask for a wired uplink; phones as hotspots as backup |
| Not enough outlets | 12 power strips plus 6 extension cords bought by Oct 5 |
| No-shows | cap registrations at 150, reconfirm Oct 3–6, waitlist |
| A minor gets hurt or lost | consent forms with emergency contacts, adult lead plus two adult volunteers on site, sign-out log, nobody leaves 22:00–07:00 without a parent |
| Overnight problems: illness, conflict, someone leaving | two adults awake all night, floor walk every 30 minutes, parent numbers at the desk, venue security briefed |
| Venue allows no overnight | Plan B daytime schedule is already written in 09-run-of-show.md |
| Judging overruns | strict 4-minute expo slots, 5 finalists only, judges briefed Oct 9 |
| Sponsor wants control | sponsor kit says what they get; nothing else |
| Cheating with pre-built code | repo history checked by judges; rule is clear at opening |
