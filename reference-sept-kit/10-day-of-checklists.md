# 10 · Day-of checklists

## Supplies to buy by Sept 30

- 12 power strips, 6 extension cords (10 m), gaffer tape 3 rolls, cable ties
- Name tags or wristbands ×150, markers ×20, lanyards if printed badges
- Printed: schedule posters ×6, table numbers ×35, direction signs ×10, judge score sheets ×60, check-in lists ×2, blank consent forms ×40, sponsor logo boards
- First-aid kit, hand sanitizer, paper towels, trash bags ×30
- 2 spare laptops, 2 HDMI cables, 1 USB-C adapter, 1 clicker, 1 portable speaker, 1 router
- Snack table: fruit, biscuits, instant noodles and cups, coffee and tea, 300 liters water total
- Overnight: quiet-zone signs, 2 sleeping-room signs, eye masks and earplugs ×100 (cheap), 2 extra first-aid kits, flashlights ×4, sign-out log book
- Prize envelopes or prize products, certificates ×40 printed with blanks for names

## Venue setup, Saturday 10:00

- [ ] Tables for 30 teams, 4 chairs each, aisles wide enough for judges to walk
- [ ] Power strip on every second table, cords taped down
- [ ] Projector, screen, mic, speaker tested with the countdown clock and the opening slides
- [ ] Wifi name and password on every table and the screen
- [ ] Check-in desk at the door with lists, tags, consent forms, pens
- [ ] Signs: entrance, bathrooms, food, mentor desk, organizer desk, quiet corner
- [ ] Snack table stocked, trash bins placed
- [ ] Lockable room for prizes and supplies; adult lead has the key
- [ ] Two sleeping rooms labeled, chairs cleared, lights dimmable, adult volunteer assigned to each
- [ ] Security guard or building manager briefed on the overnight with the adult lead's number
- [ ] Sponsor boards visible from the stage

## Check-in, 11:00

- [ ] Two lines, A–L and M–Z
- [ ] Verify name, consent form, dietary tag
- [ ] Hand out tag, point to the group chat QR and the schedule
- [ ] Late list for arrivals after 12:00, cutoff 13:30
- [ ] Headcount at 12:30 sent to the food lead

## Food

- [ ] Saturday lunch boxes at tables 13:00, dinner 18:45, midnight snack ready 23:15, breakfast 06:45, Sunday lunch at tables 12:15
- [ ] Dietary meals labeled and set aside
- [ ] Volunteers serve; no self-service crush at the table
- [ ] Water refilled every 3 hours

## Overnight, Saturday 21:00 to Sunday 07:00

- [ ] Sign-out log at the desk from 21:00; every departure matched to a parent pickup
- [ ] Night shift roster posted at the desk: who is awake 22:00–02:00 and 02:00–06:00
- [ ] Floor walk every 30 minutes, noted on the roster
- [ ] Parent phone list and consent forms at the desk; overnight-permission tags checked at 22:00
- [ ] Quiet hours 01:00–06:00 announced once, music off
- [ ] Morning: sleeping rooms cleared by 09:30, trash out, coffee out

## Judging, Sunday

- [ ] Score sheets per judge pair with table assignments
- [ ] Timekeeper volunteer per pair with a phone timer
- [ ] Tally sheet ready at 15:00; two people tally independently and compare
- [ ] Finalist list read from the stage and posted in the chat
- [ ] Prize envelopes or products in order on the stage table
- [ ] Certificates filled in during finals

## Closing

- [ ] Group photo with sponsors' boards in frame
- [ ] Survey link on screen and in the chat
- [ ] Pickups at 17:00: adult lead at the door until the last student leaves
- [ ] Sweep the room: laptops, chargers, phones left behind go to the organizer desk
- [ ] Tables and chairs back, trash out, room photographed as returned

## Post-event, Oct 12–13

- [ ] Thank-you emails with photos and numbers to sponsors, partners, judges, mentors, venue
- [ ] Results post published; project links with team permission
- [ ] Survey sent; results collected by Oct 16
- [ ] Ledger closed by the NGO, all invoices paid, copies to sponsors
- [ ] Metrics sheet filled (see 00-master-plan.md)
- [ ] Team retro: what broke, what to keep, date for next year
