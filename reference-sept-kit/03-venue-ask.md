# 03 · Venue ask

Two asks run in parallel from Monday Sept 7. Venue gate is Wednesday Sept 16.

## What we need from any venue

- Hall or open floor for 120 people at tables, plus a stage area or front wall for a projector.
- 40+ power outlets or permission to run our 12 power strips and 6 extension cords.
- Wifi that holds 150 devices, or a wired uplink we can hang a router on.
- Projector, screen, microphone, speaker.
- Continuous access from Sat Oct 10 10:00 to Sun Oct 11 18:00, overnight included, with a security guard or a way to lock the building with us inside.
- Two separate rooms or corners for sleeping (girls, boys), ideally dimmable lighting.
- If overnight is impossible: Sat 10:00–22:00 and Sun 08:00–18:00 (Plan B).
- Space for food service, bathrooms, a lockable room for supplies.
- Free, or sponsored, or a price we can put in front of a sponsor.

## A. The personal contact (likes visibility)

Talk in person or by phone first. Then send the brief below the same day.

**Script, 90 seconds:**

"I'm organizing a 24-hour hackathon for Ulaanbaatar high school students on October 10 and 11, over 100 students from 15 or more schools, free to attend, judged by engineers and teachers. It will be the largest independent student hackathon in the country. It runs 24 hours, so I need a hall for 120 people with power and wifi that we can stay in overnight from Saturday morning to Sunday afternoon, with adult supervision all night. I'm asking you for three things. One: a hall, or an introduction to the person who controls one. Two: one introduction to a company that might sponsor food or prizes. Three: a yes or no by this Friday, September 11, so I can visit the space next week.

In return, you open the event on Saturday morning with a speech, you hand out the first prize on Sunday in front of everyone, your name and photo go on the site and in the results post, and you're listed as honorary guest. Cameras will be there."

**Do not offer:** an organizing role, a judging seat, control over sponsors or the program. If he asks, the answer is "the program is set, the stage is yours."

## Visit checklist

Count outlets. Run a speed test on the wifi with 5 devices. Check projector input (HDMI or USB-C). Confirm overnight: who is responsible for the building at night, security guard, which rooms can be sleeping rooms, heating overnight, bathrooms open. Ask where food can be served. Photograph the room for the sponsor deck.
