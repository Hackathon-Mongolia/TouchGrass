# 05 · Sponsor kit

Ask deadline for commitments: Sun Sept 27. First wave Tue Sept 8, second wave Mon Sept 14, phone follow-ups every Friday.

## Targets

| Category | Examples of who to ask | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Banks | the big commercial banks' marketing or CSR teams | youth, digital, recruitment pipeline |
| Telecoms and ISPs | mobile operators, internet providers | wifi in kind, brand with young users |
| Software companies | MOSA member companies, the internship company | hiring pipeline, tech track, mentors |
| Consumer brands | food and drink companies, coffee chains | food in kind, sampling |
| Coworking and hardware shops | coworking spaces, electronics retailers | prizes and vouchers in kind |

Rule: ask the internship company first. A company that already knows the adult lead says yes fastest and often becomes title sponsor.

## Tiers

| Tier | Amount (MNT) | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Title | 8,000,000 | name in the event title ("[NAME] presented by X"), logo first everywhere, 5-minute opening slot, judge seat, branded track prize, first access to winners, CVs of consenting participants |
| Track | 3,000,000 | a named prize category ("Best use of X"), logo on site and stage, judge seat for the track, 15-minute workshop Saturday |
| Food | 4,000,000 or pay the caterer directly | "Meals by X" signage at every meal, logo on site, thank-you from stage three times |
| Prize | 1,000,000 | one named prize, logo on site, present the prize on stage |
| In-kind | goods or services | logo on site, thank-you from stage; venue, printing, water, photographer, t-shirts, cloud credits |

Every sponsor gets: logo on the site and results post, mention in the report, photos.

## One-pager content

- Title, dates, venue, "free for students, 100+ participants, 15+ schools".
- Three lines on what happens: build for 24 hours overnight, demo, judged, prizes.
- Why it matters: first independent student hackathon at this scale since 2021; a pipeline of young engineers.
- Tiers table.
- Who is behind it: organizing team, adult lead, NGO host, venue partner.
- Contact and deadline.

## What to say when they ask

- "Is it legal for us to pay students?" You don't. You pay [NGO] under a sponsorship contract and receive an e-barimt invoice for advertising services (Advertising Law art. 19). Plain donations are not deductible for companies, so we structure it as advertising.
- "Can we bring our own prize?" Yes. Product prizes are welcome and avoid prize tax paperwork.
- "Can we recruit?" You may talk to participants, run a workshop, and receive CVs of those who opt in. No mandatory data collection.
- "Can we pick the winner of our track?" You place one judge on the track panel; the rubric decides.
- "What if the event is cancelled?" Refund or roll-over, stated in the contract.

## Contract essentials (the NGO signs)

Parties; amount and payment date; benefits list exactly as the tier; logo usage; invoice as advertising services; cancellation and refund; no control over program or judging; report delivery by Oct 13.
