## Minimum viable version

If the money gate fails on Sept 27: no cash prizes, sponsors pay the caterer directly, prizes are sponsor products, printing done at school, and the overnight is dropped for the two-session daytime Plan B, which cuts food to 49,000 per person and removes the security line. Cash needed then: roughly 700,000 for supplies.

## Rules for spending

- Every payment goes from the NGO account with an e-barimt receipt kept.
- Sponsor payments are invoiced by the NGO as advertising services under a written sponsorship contract (Advertising Law art. 19).
- Keep a one-sheet ledger: date, item, amount, receipt number, who approved. Share it with sponsors after the event.
