# 09 · Run of show (24-hour overnight format, 13:00 start)

Hacking runs 24 hours: Saturday 13:00 to Sunday 13:00. Venue access needed Saturday 10:00 through Sunday 18:00 continuously. MC = lead unless noted. The countdown clock on the projector runs the full 24 hours. Time checks go out from the mic and in the group chat.

**Plan B:** if the venue refuses overnight access, run two daytime sessions instead: Sat 11:00–21:00 and Sun 09:00–17:00 with the submission deadline at Sun 13:00. Everything else in this kit still applies.

## Overnight supervision

- Minimum three adults on site overnight: the adult lead plus two adult volunteers (teachers, parents, university-age mentors over 21). Shifts: 22:00–02:00, 02:00–06:00, with the adult lead on call all night.
- One sleeping room for girls, one for boys, with an adult volunteer of the same gender assigned to each. Participants bring their own sleeping bag and mat.
- Nobody leaves the building between 22:00 and 07:00 except with a parent picking them up, logged at the desk.
- The venue's security guard, if any, is briefed on the overnight and has the adult lead's number.
- First-aid kit and a list of all parent numbers at the desk all night.

## Sleep, what to expect

Most participants sleep two to five hours between 01:00 and 07:00, in shifts within their team. About a quarter will not sleep. Say at the opening that sleeping is allowed and smart, that the quiet rooms open at 22:00, and that the night shift will wake anyone who asks at a set time.

## Mic script cues

- Every time check ends with "Push your code."
- Sponsors are thanked at opening, each meal, and awards. Nowhere else.
- If Sunday slips more than 15 minutes, cut the expo instructions short, never the judging.
