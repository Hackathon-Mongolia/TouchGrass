# 04 · NGO host ask

Why: an unregistered group cannot issue receipts, so companies cannot book sponsorship as an expense, and an under-18 cannot register an NGO alone (Civil Code 16.2: contracts by 14–18 year olds need a parent's written consent; the registrar wants founders with full capacity). A registered NGO signs, receives, pays, and reports. Target: confirmed by Mon Sept 21, hard gate Sun Sept 27.

Ask two at once. Take the first written yes.

## The ask

"We need a registered organization to be the legal host of the hackathon: sign the sponsorship contracts, receive sponsor payments into your account, pay the caterer and suppliers, issue e-barimt receipts, and be named co-organizer. We raise the sponsors, run the event, and hand you every receipt. Total flow is roughly 10 to 15 million MNT. We need the answer by September 21."

## What they get

- Co-organizer name and logo on everything, opening remarks, a seat on the judging panel.
- A pipeline: 100+ of the city's most motivated pupils, their projects, and their contact details for future programs (with consent).
- A written report with numbers and photos within a week, usable in their own donor reporting.
- Zero operational work beyond signing, paying, and bookkeeping.

## Memorandum outline (one page, signed by the NGO and the adult lead)

1. Parties and the event: name, dates, venue.
2. Roles: NGO is legal host and treasurer; organizing team runs the program; adult lead is responsible on site.
3. Money flow: sponsors pay the NGO against a sponsorship contract and invoice; the NGO pays vendors against invoices; every payment has an e-barimt; the organizing team approves spending against 02-budget.md.
4. Receipts: sponsorship is invoiced as advertising services under Advertising Law art. 19 so sponsors can book it as an expense.
5. Prizes: cash prizes paid by the NGO to winners with 5% personal income tax withheld and remitted (PIT law 19.1.1); product prizes handed over with a signed list.
6. Surplus: any unspent money stays with the NGO for youth programs, or rolls into next year's event, stated explicitly.
7. Reporting: ledger and report delivered to the NGO and every sponsor by Oct 13.
8. Liability and consent: parental consent forms collected by the organizing team and held by the adult lead; the NGO is named on the consent form as co-organizer.
9. Cancellation: if the venue or headcount gate fails, sponsors are refunded or asked to roll over, in writing.
